How to add page numbers to a PDF

A report put together from several files, a printed handout, the appendices of an application: page numbers let everyone say “see page 12” and make sure no page is missing.

Step by step

  1. Open the editor and open your PDF. It stays on your computer.
  2. If the document is made of several files, put it together first: see how to merge and reorder pages. Numbers are added to the pages as they are, so number last.
  3. Click Tools, then Page numbers.
  4. Choose where the numbers go, their style and the first number, then click Add numbers.
  5. Look at a few pages, then save.

The options

  • Where: bottom centred, bottom right, bottom left or top right. The numbers sit about one centimetre from the edge of the page.
  • Style: 1, 2, 3…, 1 / 10, or Page 1 of 10 (in the language the editor is shown in). The total counts the numbered pages.
  • First number: useful when the PDF is the second part of a longer document, or an appendix that must start at 25.
  • Not on the first page (cover): the cover gets no number.

A cover page: counted or not?

Books and reports do it both ways, so the editor lets you choose.

  • The cover does not count: tick Not on the first page and keep the first number at 1. The page after the cover shows 1.
  • The cover counts but shows no number: tick Not on the first page and set the first number to 2. The page after the cover shows 2, as in most printed reports.

Choosing the right place

Before you choose, look at the bottom of a few pages. Many documents already have a footer, such as an address line, a reference or a confidentiality notice, within a centimetre of the edge. If the number would land on it, choose Top right instead. For a document printed on both sides, the bottom centre is the safest choice: it is in the same place on left and right pages.

When the PDF already has page numbers

A merged PDF often has the old numbers of each part: 1 to 4, then 1 to 9. Adding new numbers in another corner keeps both, which is confusing. Remove the old ones first: with the Edit text tool, click an old number and delete it, or cover it with Whiteout. Then add the new numbers.

Mistakes and how to fix them

  • Wrong position or style. Use undo (⌘Z on a Mac, Ctrl+Z elsewhere) and add them again. Do not add a second set on top.
  • Pages added after numbering. The new pages have no number and the totals are wrong. Undo the numbering, add the pages, then number again.
  • Scanned pages. Numbers work on scans too: they are drawn on top of the picture.
