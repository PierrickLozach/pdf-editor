This site uses no cookies: no advertising, analytics, social network or tracking cookies, and no third-party cookies.

This site uses advertising cookies only if you agree: on the home page, Google AdSense may store cookies to show and measure one ad. If you refuse, or until you choose, no cookie is stored. There are no analytics or social network cookies.

Advertising cookies (with your consent)

If you accept, Google may store cookies such as __gads, __gpi and IDE for up to 13 months. Your choice itself is kept for 6 months in a small cookie set by the consent banner, so that it does not come back on every page; after 6 months you are asked again. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.

What is stored in your browser

The editor keeps the document you are editing, the list of recent files, the fonts you add and your preferences (such as your language) in your browser's local storage. To count visits anonymously, each tab also notes that it has already been counted; this note is deleted when the tab closes and identifies nothing. This is only used to provide the editor you are using, stays on your device and is never read by us, so it needs no consent. See the privacy page.
