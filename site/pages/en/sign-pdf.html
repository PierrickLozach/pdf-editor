How to sign a PDF without printing it

Print, sign, scan, send: there is a faster way, and it gives a cleaner result. Here is how to add your signature directly to a PDF.

Step by step

  1. Open the editor and open the PDF to sign.
  2. Click Tools, then Sign.
  3. Create your signature in one of three ways:
    • Draw: sign with the mouse, a trackpad, a finger on a touch screen, or a stylus. A stylus gives the most natural line.
    • Type: type your name; it is written in a handwriting font.
    • Upload: use a photo or scan of your signature on white paper. The paper is made transparent, so only the ink remains.
  4. Click on the page where the signature should go. Drag it to move it, and drag a corner to resize it.
  5. Press Enter or click Add, then save the PDF.

Your signatures are kept in this browser, on this device, so next time you can place one in a single click. They are never sent anywhere. To remove them, clear the site's data in your browser settings.

Tips for a clean result

  • Sign larger than needed, then make it smaller on the page: the line looks finer.
  • For a photo of a paper signature, use dark ink, plain white paper and good light, and crop it close to the ink.
  • Fill in the date and your name next to the signature with Add text.

What is this signature worth?

A signature added this way is an image of your handwritten signature. In the European Union it counts as a simple electronic signature under the eIDAS regulation: it is generally accepted for everyday documents such as forms, quotes, delivery notes or letters, and a court cannot reject it only because it is electronic.

It does not prove by itself who signed, and it does not lock the document. Some documents require an advanced or qualified electronic signature, based on a certificate tied to your identity: for those, use a trust service provider. When in doubt, ask the person who will receive the document what they accept.
