Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no watermark, no page limit. The editor runs on your computer, so it costs almost nothing to offer.

Is it really free?

Yes, everyday editing is free: no trial, no watermark, no page limit, no account. The editor runs on your computer, so it costs almost nothing to offer. Optional Pro features (batch processing, smart redaction, unlimited OCR, comparing PDFs, forms) are paid.

What do I get with Pro?

Batch processing, automatic redaction of personal data, text recognition without a page limit, comparing two PDFs, and forms. Your files stay on your computer, as with the free editor. A licence works on one computer at a time; you can move it to another one from the Pro window.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the editor and drop a PDF or a document.

Does Save change my original file?

In Chrome and Edge, it can: “Save (overwrites)” writes your changes into the PDF you opened, like any desktop app, but asks first, so you can save a new file instead. “Download copy” never touches your original, and in Firefox and Safari Save downloads a copy too. “Recent files” reopens a saved PDF in one click; Undo works until you close the PDF.

How large can a PDF be?

Up to 250 MB. Larger PDFs are almost always scans or photos: use “Reduce file size…” in the editor, or on a Mac, Preview › File › Export › Quartz Filter: Reduce File Size.

Will my edits look like the original?

In most PDFs, yes: the editor reuses the fonts inside the PDF, then the fonts on your computer, then free fonts of the same family. When a letter is truly unavailable it uses a look-alike with the same width, and tells you.

Can I edit scanned documents?

A scan is a picture of a page, without text to edit. You can still white out parts and add text and images on top.

Where are my fonts kept?

In your browser, on this device. They are never sent anywhere. Only add fonts you are allowed to use.

Can I edit Word documents?

Yes. Open a Word (.docx) or LibreOffice (.odt) file in the editor as you would a PDF: edit it on real pages and save it, in your browser as well. To edit the text of a PDF as a document, use Tools › Convert › Edit as a document.
