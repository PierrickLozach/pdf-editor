Private by design

Most online PDF editors upload your documents to their servers. This one can't: the whole editor runs inside your browser.

  • Your PDFs are opened, edited and saved on your device only — never uploaded.
  • No account, no cookies, no ads, no tracking.
  • Nothing about you is stored on a server: your fonts and settings stay in your browser.
  • Free and open source: anyone can check what it does.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no watermark, no page limit. The editor runs on your computer, so it costs almost nothing to offer.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the editor and drop a PDF.

Does Save change my original file?

In Chrome and Edge, yes: Save writes your changes into the PDF you opened, like any desktop app, and “Recent files” lets you reopen it in one click. In Firefox and Safari, Save downloads the edited PDF and your original stays as it was. Undo works until you close the PDF.

How large can a PDF be?

Up to 250 MB. Larger PDFs are almost always scans or photos: use “Reduce file size…” in the editor, or on a Mac, Preview › File › Export › Quartz Filter: Reduce File Size.

Will my edits look like the original?

In most PDFs, yes: the editor reuses the fonts inside the PDF, then the fonts on your computer, then free fonts of the same family. When a letter is truly unavailable it uses a look-alike with the same width, and tells you.

Can I edit scanned documents?

A scan is a picture of a page, without text to edit. You can still white out parts and add text and images on top.

Where are my fonts kept?

In your browser, on this device. They are never sent anywhere. Only add fonts you are allowed to use.
