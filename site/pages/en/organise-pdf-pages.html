How to merge, split and reorder PDF pages

Put a contract and its appendices in one file, send only pages 3 to 5, turn a sideways scan the right way up: all of this happens in the page panel.

The page panel

When a PDF is open, its pages appear as thumbnails on the left. Point at a thumbnail to see its buttons: Rotate 90° clockwise, Duplicate page, Insert a blank page after and Delete page. To move a page, drag its thumbnail to its new place.

Merge several PDFs

  1. Open the first PDF.
  2. Click + Add pages from a PDF… under the thumbnails, and choose the next PDF: its pages are added at the end.
  3. Repeat for each document, then drag thumbnails to put the pages in the right order.
  4. Save, or download the combined PDF under a new name.

Extract or split pages

  1. Click Tools, then Split or extract pages.
  2. Type the pages you want, for example 1-3, 5, 8- (8- means “from page 8 to the end”).
  3. Choose what to create: Extract pages into one new PDF, One PDF per range, or One PDF per page. Several files are downloaded together in a .zip archive.

The open PDF is not changed: the extracted pages go into new files.

Good to know

  • Every page action can be undone with ⌘Z or Ctrl+Z.
  • Rotating a page only changes how it is displayed and printed. Its content, including text you can search, is unchanged.
  • Pictures can become a PDF too: on the start screen, Create a PDF from pictures turns photos or scans into a PDF, one picture per page.
