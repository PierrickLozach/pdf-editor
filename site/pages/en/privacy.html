Your documents

The editor runs entirely in your web browser. PDF files you open are read, edited and saved on your device; they are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What your browser keeps

To work well, the editor stores a few things in your browser's own storage, on your device only: the document you are editing (so that a reload does not lose your work – “Close” removes it), the list of recent files (a reference to each file on your computer, never a copy), the fonts you add or that it downloaded, and small preferences such as your language. We cannot see any of it. You can delete it at any time from your browser's settings (site data).

Word and LibreOffice documents

Word and LibreOffice documents are handled the same way: they are opened, edited and saved in your browser and never uploaded. The word processor the editor uses for them (EdenText) has an AI assistant and dictation, which would send your text or your voice to another service: both are switched off on this site, and the site's security settings allow it no connection to any other website. Spell-check dictionaries are downloaded from this site.

Recent documents are in the same list as PDFs (a reference to each file, never a copy). While a document has unsaved changes, your browser keeps a copy of it, deleted once you save, discard or close it. The visit statistics only count that a document was opened, saved, downloaded or created, whether it is a Word or LibreOffice file, and the format of a download.

Free fonts

When a PDF uses a free font (for example from Google Fonts) and the letters you type are missing from the PDF, the editor downloads that font from the jsDelivr network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Like any download, this request reveals your network (IP) address and the font's name to jsDelivr — never your document. You can switch this off in the editor's Fonts window.

Visit statistics

To know how much the site is used, it counts visits itself – no analytics company is involved. When a page opens, it tells our server which page it is; for the first page of a visit, also the website that sent you (its domain name only, for example “google”), your browser's language, and whether you use a computer, a tablet or a phone. Our server adds the country it sees for the connection. In the editor, it also counts that a PDF or a document was opened, saved, downloaded or created, the size range of a PDF, whether a document is a Word or LibreOffice file and the format it was downloaded in, which tools were used, and when the word processor had to be restarted – never a file name, nor anything from your documents.

Only totals per day are kept (for example “French visitors on 3 March: 12”). Your IP address, a cookie or any identifier are never stored or used, so a visit cannot be linked to you or to another visit. Nothing is counted if your browser asks websites not to track you (Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control).

Pro licences

Buying. Payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy, LLC (United States), the reseller and merchant of record: it processes your name, email address, country, billing address and payment details to take the payment, apply VAT, issue the invoice and send you the licence key, under its own privacy policy. We receive the details of your order from it (name, email, plan, dates) to manage your licence, answer your requests and meet our accounting obligations. Legal basis: performing the contract; kept for the life of the licence, then 10 years for accounting records.

Activating. When you enter a key, the site records this computer's random identifier (made by your browser, it says nothing about you) and the kind of browser (for example “Chrome · Mac”), so that a licence works on one computer at a time. They are deleted when you release the computer or when the licence ends. Your browser keeps the key and a signed proof of the licence, renewed every few days.

Order data may be transferred to the United States (Lemon Squeezy, Cloudflare), under the safeguards of the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or the EU–US framework).
