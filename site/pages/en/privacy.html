Privacy

Last updated: {{updated}}

In short: we do not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

Your documents

The editor runs entirely in your web browser. PDF files you open are read, edited and saved on your device; they are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What your browser keeps

To work well, the editor stores a few things in your browser's own storage, on your device only: the document you are editing (so that a reload does not lose your work – “Close” removes it), the fonts you add or that it downloaded, and small preferences such as your language. We cannot see any of it. You can delete it at any time from your browser's settings (site data).

Free fonts

When a PDF uses a free font (for example from Google Fonts) and the letters you type are missing from the PDF, the editor downloads that font from the jsDelivr network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Like any download, this request reveals your network (IP) address and the font's name to jsDelivr — never your document. You can switch this off in the editor's Fonts window.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc., which processes technical data (such as IP addresses) to deliver the pages and protect them against attacks, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or keep these records.

Your rights

As we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. For any question, write to {{publisher.email}}. You can also contact the CNIL (www.cnil.fr) or the data protection authority of your country.
