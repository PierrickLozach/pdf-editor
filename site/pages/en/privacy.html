Privacy

Last updated: {{updated}}

In short: we do not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no cookies and no ads, and no analytics company is involved. We only count visits anonymously, in daily totals, to know how much the site is used.

Your documents

The editor runs entirely in your web browser. PDF files you open are read, edited and saved on your device; they are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What your browser keeps

To work well, the editor stores a few things in your browser's own storage, on your device only: the document you are editing (so that a reload does not lose your work – “Close” removes it), the list of recent files (a reference to each file on your computer, never a copy), the fonts you add or that it downloaded, and small preferences such as your language. We cannot see any of it. You can delete it at any time from your browser's settings (site data).

Free fonts

When a PDF uses a free font (for example from Google Fonts) and the letters you type are missing from the PDF, the editor downloads that font from the jsDelivr network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Like any download, this request reveals your network (IP) address and the font's name to jsDelivr — never your document. You can switch this off in the editor's Fonts window.

Visit statistics

To know how much the site is used, it counts visits itself – no analytics company is involved. When a page opens, it tells our server which page it is; for the first page of a visit, also the website that sent you (its domain name only, for example “google”), your browser's language, and whether you use a computer, a tablet or a phone. Our server adds the country it sees for the connection. In the editor, it also counts that a PDF was opened, saved or created, its size range and which tools were used – never a file name, nor anything from your documents.

Only totals per day are kept (for example “French visitors on 3 March: 12”). Your IP address, a cookie or any identifier are never stored or used, so a visit cannot be linked to you or to another visit. Nothing is counted if your browser asks websites not to track you (Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control).

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc., which processes technical data (such as IP addresses) to deliver the pages and protect them against attacks, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or keep these records.

Your rights

As we hold no data about you (the statistics are anonymous totals), there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. For any question, write to {{publisher.email}}. You can also contact the CNIL (www.cnil.fr) or the data protection authority of your country.
