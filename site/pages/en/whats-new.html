What's new

{{name}} is improved every week. The newest changes come first; the complete history is on GitHub.

Word and LibreOffice documents 23 November 2026

  • The editor now also opens, edits and saves Word (.docx) and LibreOffice (.odt) documents, with real pages, styles, tables, track changes, comments and spell check. Open or drop them like a PDF: they are in the same Recent files list, and nothing is uploaded.
  • Tools › Convert › Edit as a document turns a PDF's text into a document you can change.

Phones and saving 22 November 2026

  • On phones, the toolbar no longer scrolls sideways: a “⋯” button stays in view and lists everything else, including Sign, Redact and OCR. Toolbar buttons are larger on touch screens.
  • When Save writes into the file you opened (Chrome, Edge), the button now says “Save (overwrites)”; “Download copy” always leaves your original as it was.
  • The font window no longer shows a sentence with an empty font name.

Offline editing and guides 18 November 2026

  • Editing now also works without an internet connection once the editor is loaded: letters missing from a PDF's own font are drawn with fonts built into the editor.
  • An edit that cannot write every letter is refused with a clear message, instead of leaving a letter out.
  • New: practical guides, an About page and a Contact page.

Certificates and whiteout 16 November 2026

  • Some PDFs, such as generated certificates, draw their text in a layer above the page. That text can now be edited, whited out and redacted like any other.
  • The home page shows a short video of an edit.

A domain of its own 15 November 2026

  • The editor moved to quicktou.ch. Old links keep working.
  • Convert a PDF to Markdown or to an e-book (EPUB), with headings, lists and pictures.
  • The close button now sits at the top right of the document, like closing a file.

Compare and languages 14 November 2026

  • Compare two versions of a PDF word by word: removed words in red, added words in green, at their place on the page.
  • Change the language from the editor's toolbar without closing your PDF.
  • The “Saved” or “Not saved” note stays readable even with a long file name.

Removing items 12 November 2026

  • Click a stamp, a watermark or a picture to delete it, on one page or on every page.
  • A new start screen with a three-step guide, and a bigger “Choose a PDF” button.

The toolbox 10 November 2026

  • Sign, redact, make scans searchable (OCR), split, convert, protect with a password, add page numbers and watermarks.
  • A free JavaScript library for developers.
  • Save writes the changes back into your file in Chrome and Edge, and the saved PDF keeps the original's properties.
  • 12 languages, including Arabic and Hebrew.

First version 9 November 2026

  • Change the existing text of a PDF in its original font, entirely in the browser.
